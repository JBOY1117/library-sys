Jerome Geraldo

Jerome Geraldo is the author of all the works presented in this library. A storyteller at heart, he weaves worlds across genres — from the slow dread of gothic horror to the pulse of high-octane action.

Every story is original. Every character is crafted with care. Every page is an invitation to feel something true.
